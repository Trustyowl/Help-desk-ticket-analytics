\# Help Desk Ticket Analytics Findings



\## Project Summary



This project analyzes simulated IT help desk ticket data using SQL, SQLite, Python, pandas, and matplotlib. The goal is to identify ticket patterns, resolution performance, SLA compliance, department support needs, and technician performance.



\## Key Findings



1\. Ticket volume varies across departments and issue categories.



2\. Password resets, software issues, network problems, hardware issues, and account lockouts represent common help desk requests.



3\. High-priority and critical tickets generally require closer monitoring because they have greater business impact.



4\. Some tickets exceeded their SLA targets, demonstrating the importance of tracking response and resolution performance.



5\. Resolution times differ depending on the type of technical issue.



6\. Technician performance can be compared using ticket volume, average resolution time, SLA performance, and satisfaction scores.



7\. Customer satisfaction provides an additional metric for evaluating the quality of technical support.



\## Recommendations



\- Monitor frequently occurring ticket categories to identify opportunities for automation or self-service documentation.

\- Track SLA misses and investigate the causes of delayed resolutions.

\- Use technician performance metrics together rather than relying on ticket volume alone.

\- Monitor department-level ticket volume to identify areas that may require additional training or IT resources.
